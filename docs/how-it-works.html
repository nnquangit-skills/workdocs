How it works

One word in the chat becomes six pages in docs/, in eight steps the skill prescribes and the agent reports on.

Big picture

You say one word in the chat. The agent loads the skill, reads the project, writes the pages and checks them. The reader opens a folder.

Source: SKILL.md "Run", assets/

Step by step

One run of workdocs on a project, in the order SKILL.md prescribes. The diagram walks through the steps below on its own.

  1. Trigger — you say workdocs; the agent loads SKILL.md and the references it names (SKILL.md, first paragraph).
  2. Survey — README, manifest, entry points, handlers, auth, config, environment example and test scripts, with targeted searches and ranged reads (Run step 1).
  3. Inventory — each of the six files is missing, workdocs-managed (markers present) or foreign/legacy (Run step 2).
  4. Discover helpers — token-save, a diagram skill, playwright-cli, a Remotion skill; check whether workmap is installed under the commercial rule (Run step 3).
  5. Draft — all six pages from references/pages.md, keeping a claim-to-source list; a page that does not apply says what is absent (Run step 4, Hard rule 4).
  6. Write — existing pages are backed up to docs/.workdocs-backup/, then the pages are written; workdocs.css and workdocs.js are copied only when absent (Run step 5, references/static.md).
  7. Diagrams and motion — the architecture diagram on Overview, the animated flow and the step player on How it works, the step player on Demo script, all from real call sites and the blocks in assets/snippets.html (Run step 6).
  8. QA — docs/ served alone: console, requests, light and dark, 375 px, navigation, markers, links; browser checks marked unverified when no browser is available (Run step 7, references/qa.md).
  9. Report — pages created, replaced, untouched; backups; evidence gaps; checks run; skills loaded; workmap status and, when it is absent, the pointer line (Run step 8).

Failure paths

  • No evidence for a page (no API, no auth): the page is still written and says what is absent and what exists instead; nothing is invented (Hard rule 4).
  • Foreign or legacy pages: backed up in full to docs/.workdocs-backup/static-<timestamp>/, checksums confirmed, then migrated; unique accurate details are carried forward, stale claims dropped (references/static.md).
  • Existing assets differ from the skill's copies: they are preserved and the mismatch is reported (Run step 5).
  • No browser available: the static checks run and every browser check is listed as unverified (Run step 7).
  • workmap absent or unreadable: no work-map page or nav entry, the pointer card on the Overview, the pointer line in the report; never recreated (SKILL.md, "Work map").
  • Storage blocked (file://, private window): the theme follows the system; the toggle still works for the open page (workdocs.js, store() in a try/catch).

Real vs simulated

Nothing in the skill is mocked. Two things are presentation only: the animated flow and the step player move, but the content is the HTML underneath, readable with JavaScript off. The names in assets/snippets.html (Storefront, Gateway, Payments) belong to a fictional shop and are placeholders the agent must replace with the project's real components.
